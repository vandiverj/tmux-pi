# Counterpoint (explicit, read-only)

Use when the human asks to challenge a plan, design, decision, or recommendation. This is a focused second perspective, **not** a mandatory stage for every task and not a replacement for tests or required independent review.

1. Restate the favored decision and the evidence actually supporting it.
2. Pick the one or two most relevant lenses: user/operator fit, simpler alternative, incorrect root cause, maintenance, security/privacy, cost, dependency, reversibility, or failure mode. Avoid a generic checklist.
3. Build the strongest credible objection and a specific alternative. Distinguish observed facts from assumptions; name what evidence would change the choice. Challenge sunk-cost reasoning where relevant.
4. Reconcile: **keep**, **modify**, **pause for one missing fact**, or **replace**, with the tradeoff. Do not manufacture disagreement or force consensus.

Output a concise counterpoint, evidence gap, alternative, and recommendation. For consequential decisions, add a short impact/next-check section. Do not silently change an artifact, approve a decision, start implementation, invoke tools, or waive human approval merely because this skill was selected. A human-requested counterpoint is not edit authority.
