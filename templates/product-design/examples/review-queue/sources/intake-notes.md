# Intake notes (synthetic)

Synthetic demonstration material for the tmux-pi product design fixture. Not from any real product.

## Submission

A requester submits a record for review. Every submission needs a title and a category; reviewers cannot triage a
record without both.

## Review

Each submission is assigned to one reviewer, who resolves it as approved or returned. Assignment can change while a
review is in progress; nobody has said what happens to the reviewer's unsaved work when it does.
