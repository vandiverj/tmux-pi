# Skill title

State the authority boundary first: this skill is task guidance only and grants no edit, tool, approval or deployment authority.

1. Say what to do, as standing guidance rather than one-time steps.
2. Keep one job per skill. Keep the body under about 150 lines.
3. Describe the expected output.

Catalogue skills stay single-file. For a plugin-style skill, link a reference one hop away, such as `references/details.md`, and keep it free of further links.
